Nanacon · My chibi girl - Dress up game (최애소녀)

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains how Nanacon and the services in My chibi girl - Dress up game (Korean title: 최애소녀) for Android (package com.ggee.mychibireboot), a single-player anime dress-up game, handle information when you play the game or see an ad.

Gameplay and device storage

The game does not require an account and does not use a game server. Your current outfit and background, saved outfit slots and the memos you write for them, and your music and sound-effect volume are stored only on your device so you can continue where you left off. Nanacon does not receive this information. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game removes it.

Ads and diagnostics

We use Unity Ads to show and measure full-screen ads in the game. Unity Ads may collect or share approximate location inferred from network information, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and other in-app activity related to ads, information about apps installed on the device used for ad delivery and measurement, and diagnostics. Unity uses this information to serve and measure ads, operate its services, and detect fraud. The Unity SDK transmits the data it collects using encryption in transit.

Advertising preferences and identifiers can be managed in Android's privacy settings. Availability and names of controls depend on the Android version and region. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for information about Unity's processing and privacy choices.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Sharing and retention

Unity and its advertising partners process advertising and diagnostic information and apply their own retention periods. Nanacon does not sell personal information and does not otherwise share information about you. Game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game, subject to any device backup settings.

Audience

The game is intended for players age 18 and older and is not directed to children. A parent or guardian may contact us with any concern about a child's information.

Request data deletion

  1. Email nanacon.help@gmail.com with the subject “My chibi girl data deletion.”
  2. Tell us whether your request concerns advertising data or support correspondence. Include only the details needed to locate the information.

You can delete your saved outfits, memos and settings by clearing this app's storage in Android settings. You can ask for deletion of support correspondence through the address above. For advertising identifiers, ad activity, or diagnostics held by Unity, use Unity's privacy request channels linked above, or contact us for help finding the relevant channel.

You can also use the same Nanacon email address to ask privacy questions or request access to or correction of information related to the game.

We will update the date above when we make changes to this policy.
