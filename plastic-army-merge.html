Nanacon · Plastic Army Merge

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Nanacon and the services in Plastic Army Merge for Android (package com.nanacon.plasticarmymerge), a single-player merge battle game in which plastic toy soldiers face wind-up tin robots, handle information when you play the game, see an ad, or use the in-game shop.

Gameplay and device storage

The game does not require an account and does not use a game server; you can play offline. The game stores your army, stage progress, settings, and purchase entitlements on your device so you can resume play and restore eligible items. Clearing the app's storage removes that local progress.

Ads and diagnostics

We use Unity LevelPlay to deliver and measure ads from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, including optional ads that grant in-game rewards and full-screen interstitial ads. The advertising services may collect or share approximate location inferred from network information, device and advertising identifiers, SDK identifiers, ad interactions, purchase signals, and diagnostics. They use this information to serve and measure ads, operate their services, and detect fraud. The Unity SDKs transmit the data they collect using encryption in transit.

Advertising preferences and identifiers can be managed in Android's privacy settings. Availability and names of controls depend on the Android version and region. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for information about Unity's processing and privacy choices.

Optional purchases

Google Play Billing processes payments for optional in-game items, such as gem packs, ad removal, and a starter pack. Unity In-App Purchasing handles purchase and entitlement information so the game can deliver items and restore eligible purchases. Unity's purchase services may also process SDK identifiers and performance information to operate and diagnose purchases. Google handles payment-method details under Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and retention

Unity and its advertising partners process advertising and diagnostic information. Google and Unity process purchase information for billing and entitlement purposes. Service providers apply their own retention periods. Game progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game, subject to any device backup settings.

Audience

The game is intended for players age 13 and older. A parent or guardian may contact us with any concern about a child's information.

Request data deletion

  1. Email nanaconcto@gmail.com with the subject “Plastic Army Merge data deletion.”
  2. Tell us whether your request concerns advertising data, purchase information, or support correspondence. Include only the details needed to locate the information.

You can delete local game progress and settings by clearing this app's storage in Android settings. You can ask for deletion of support correspondence through the address above. For advertising identifiers, ad activity, diagnostics, or store transaction records held by Unity or Google, use the providers' privacy request channels linked above, or contact us for help finding the relevant channel. The providers' retention periods apply to those records, including transaction data they must keep to meet legal obligations.

You can also use the same Nanacon email address to ask privacy questions or request access to or correction of information related to the game.

We will update the date above when we make changes to this policy.
